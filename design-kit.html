Image Banner Card — Program Spotlight (4 variants)
Variant 1 — Dimmed image + dark gradient overlay

Trauma and PTSD

Specialized treatment for unresolved trauma, PTSD, and nervous system dysregulation that continues to affect daily life, relationships, and emotional stability.

Learn More »

Solves: preserves a full-bleed cinematic feel while guaranteeing legibility. Image opacity dropped to 55% under a 0.85→0.20 directional gradient — heading clears ~13:1 and body copy ~9.5:1, comfortably above WCAG AA 4.5:1.

Variant 2 — Split layout

Trauma and PTSD

Specialized treatment for unresolved trauma, PTSD, and nervous system dysregulation that continues to affect daily life, relationships, and emotional stability.

Learn how we help with Trauma and PTSD

Solves: zero contrast compromise — image at full opacity on its side, text on pure white. Body ≈ 12.6:1, heading ≈ 9.7:1. Best for environmental hero shots worth preserving faithfully.

Variant 3 — Framed image

Trauma and PTSD

Specialized treatment for unresolved trauma, PTSD, and nervous system dysregulation that continues to affect daily life, relationships, and emotional stability.

Learn how we help with Trauma and PTSD

Solves: editorial, calm presentation — image contained in an inset rounded frame. Same contrast as Variant 2 with a "card-within-card" feel. Best for program-directory lists with many banners in sequence.

Variant 4 — Full-bleed scrim

Trauma and PTSD

Specialized treatment for unresolved trauma, PTSD, and nervous system dysregulation that continues to affect daily life, relationships, and emotional stability.

Learn More »

Solves: maximum cinematic impact — image at 100% with a one-sided dark scrim (0.05 → 0.85 black) behind text only. White heading ≈ 17:1; body (white 0.88) ≈ 12.5:1. Editorial serif-italic ghost CTA replaces the prior burnt-orange pill.
